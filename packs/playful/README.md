# Playful

A deliberately contrasting pack (D39): warm, rounded and energetic. It suits children's lessons, playful explainers and light-hearted ads. It shows that "style is data": the same IR renders under this pack or the neutral default by changing only the project's pack. It differs from the neutral pack in type, palette, motion grammar, transitions and sound palette, and meets the same craft bar: every contrast pair, minimum text size and safe area.

| Domain | Choice |
| --- | --- |
| Color | A warm cream background, deep violet ink and a raspberry accent. Six saturated data colours, each at least 4.2:1 on both backgrounds. |
| Type | Fredoka (variable, OFL-1.1). A larger scale than the neutral pack, from caption (2.8% of the shorter side) to display. |
| Layout | Centred compositions on a 12 × 6 (landscape) and 4 × 8 (portrait) grid, with large corner radii. |
| Motion | `pop` enabled, with an overshoot easing for emphasis only (never on body text). Words reveal one by one. |
| Transitions | Wipe by default; a cross-dissolve when time passes. |
| Sound | An upbeat ukulele-and-percussion bed at 110–140 BPM, ducked 10 dB under the voice. Soft pops and chimes are enabled. |
